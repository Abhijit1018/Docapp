A Passion for Excellence

Dr. Alexandra Chen is a board-certified dentist with over 15 years of experience in cosmetic and restorative dentistry. After graduating with honors from Stanford University School of Dentistry, she completed her residency at UCLA, specializing in advanced cosmetic procedures and dental implantology.

Throughout her career, Dr. Chen has remained committed to continuing education, attending prestigious conferences and training programs worldwide. She combines her extensive expertise with a gentle, compassionate approach that puts even the most anxious patients at ease.

DDS, Stanford University Board Certified Top Dentist 2024 Innovation Award

Philosophy of Care

"Every patient deserves to feel heard, comfortable, and confident. I believe in treating people, not just teeth — taking the time to understand your unique needs and creating personalized solutions that last. A beautiful smile begins with trust, and trust is built through genuine care and exceptional results."

— Dr. Alexandra Chen
