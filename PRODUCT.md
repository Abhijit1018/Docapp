# Product context

register: product (the front desk) with one brand surface (the clinic's public site)

## What this is

A working demo that Soulplex Studios opens in front of a clinic owner to sell two things at once: the clinic's own website with real online booking, and a simple front-desk screen for appointments and patient records. It replaces serenity.soulplexstudios.tech.

The demo is the pitch. A doctor should watch a booking made on a phone appear on the front-desk screen a second later, and see a WhatsApp reminder go out, with their own clinic's name on all of it.

## Who uses it

**In the pitch (the buyer).** A doctor who owns a one-to-three-chair clinic in a tier-2 Indian city: dentist, physiotherapist, dermatologist, paediatrician, general physician. Usually 35 to 55, runs the practice themselves, gets patients from Google Maps and word of mouth. Has a receptionist with a paper register or an Excel sheet. Has seen Practo and thinks it is expensive. Judges software in the first twenty seconds, on their own phone.

**Patients.** All ages, on a mid-range Android phone, often outdoors on mobile data, often booking for a parent or a child. Many prefer Hindi or Gujarati. They want to know three things: is the doctor in today, what does it cost, and can I come at 6.

**The receptionist.** At the desk from opening to closing with the phone ringing. On a cheap laptop or a tablet. Not technical. Needs today's list, who has arrived, who is next, and a fast way to add a walk-in or a phone booking.

## Tone

Plain, warm, specific. A good receptionist talking, not a hospital brochure. "Dr. Shah is in today till 8 PM" rather than "Experience world-class care".

## Anti-references

- The current Serenity demo: sage green, Cormorant Garamond with an italic accent word, an American doctor, an 11-field form.
- Stock photos of a smiling model with a stethoscope.
- "Healthcare teal" on white, the palette of every hospital site.
- Dribbble-style admin dashboards with eight stat cards and three charts.
- Digital visiting cards (apnabusinesscard) and keyword-stuffed headlines ("Best Dentist in Vadodara | Painless | Affordable").

## Principles

1. Show the product working; do not describe it.
2. The next free slot is the headline.
3. A booking takes three taps and under 90 seconds on a phone.
4. The front desk shows today first. Everything else is one click away.
5. Indian by default: ₹ fees, +91 numbers, WhatsApp, Hindi and Gujarati, no insurance language.
6. Every patient in the demo is fictional and says so.
