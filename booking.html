Frequently Asked Questions

Your first visit will include a comprehensive examination, digital X-rays if needed, and a consultation with Dr. Chen to discuss your oral health goals. We'll create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Regular checkups and cleanings typically take 45-60 minutes. More complex procedures or new patient exams may take 90 minutes. We'll always give you an accurate time estimate when booking.

We accept most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, and United Healthcare. Our team will help you understand your benefits and maximize your coverage.

We understand plans change. Please contact us at least 24 hours before your appointment to cancel or reschedule at no charge. We value your time and appreciate advance notice.
